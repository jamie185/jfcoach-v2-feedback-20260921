What changed

  • The nutrition target card at the top of the page now carries the food accent, so the page no longer opens grey on grey.
  • Meal actions are consistent: navy for "Swap meal" and "Mark eaten", food green for "View recipe". The red "Substitute" wording is gone.
  • The meal guide banner names the real route: tap +, choose Nutrition, scroll down.
  • One filter rail on the swap sheet, with a Filters chip that counts active filters and opens a sectioned panel.
  • "Any cal" is no longer a chip. No calorie chip simply means any calories.
  • Swap results show a radio tick and a quiet Preview pill, and the confirm bar shows calories and macros before you commit.
  • Recipe detail shows a plain dish name under a photo card, with amounts in grams and millilitres.
  • Food tracker buttons reduced to one navy primary, one grey secondary and a quiet Done. Naming a meal is now its own step.
  • Secret challenges are gold with a key, so they no longer blend into this week's challenge.
  • Home and Challenge share the same "Edit habits" chip.

How it was checked

  • Run against a local mock API loaded with fixture data, so nothing touched live records.
  • Every screen captured in Playwright driving Chrome at 390 by 844, the iPhone size, at 2x.
  • The TypeScript check passed with no errors.
  • The meal-substitute unit tests passed.
  • The nutrition unit tests passed.
  • The production build was run and completed.

Nutrition page top

The first thing a client sees when they open Nutrition.

The target card now carries the food accent: a green icon tile, an eyebrow above the heading, and macro tiles with colour dots. The top of the page is no longer grey on grey, so the daily target reads as the headline of the screen.

Meal guide entry and cards

How a client finds the meal guide, and what the meal cards offer once they are there.

"Substitute" in red read like a delete button, so people avoided it. The actions are now navy Swap meal and Mark eaten with icons, and View recipe uses the food green. The sparkle tile became a fork and knife, and the wayfinding banner names the real route: tap +, choose Nutrition, scroll down.

Swap sheet and filters

Choosing a different meal, and narrowing the list down to something a client will actually cook.

There is now one filter rail with a Filters chip that shows how many filters are on and opens a sectioned panel: Meal, Calories, Time and favourites, Cuisine, Diet and ingredients to avoid, and Keep the dish. "Any cal" is gone as a chip, because no calorie chip already means any calories. The rail fades out with a chevron so it is obviously scrollable. Result rows have a radio tick and a quiet Preview pill, the confirm bar shows calories and macros before you commit, and the toast shows the change afterwards.

Recipe detail

Opening a recipe from the meal guide.

There is no before shot for this screen, so only the after is shown. The before state was the long imported recipe name printed over the photo, which was hard to read. The recipe now shows a plain dish name below a photo card, and ingredient amounts show grams and millilitres instead of ounces, unless a client has chosen pounds in Settings.

Food tracker, saved step

What a client sees straight after logging food, and when they save a combination as a named meal.

Three differently coloured buttons, navy, pale green and grey, became one navy primary, one grey secondary and a quiet Done. Naming a meal is now its own step with Save meal as the primary action, and the header says Close, because the food is already saved by that point. The duplicate Done in the header was removed, so the full-width Save button under the results is the one submit.

Challenge

The weekly challenge hero and the task rows underneath it.

The secret challenge row is now gold with a key, so it no longer blends into this week's challenge, and the unlocked-secret notice shares the same gold tile. The underlined "Build your habit" text became the same Edit habits chip used on Home, and the community notice carries a red badge and a chevron so it reads as something to tap.

Home

The My Day block on the home screen.

An Edit habits chip now sits beside My Day, matching the one on the Challenge page, so there is a single recognisable control for habits wherever a client meets them.

Map trainer challenge

Tapping a club on the JF Map and finding the trainer's challenge inside it.

There was no polished trainer bubble before, so only the club view exists as a comparison. The trainer challenge bubble now uses the app font, a chevron and a pointer to its trainer, and tapping it opens the challenge sheet.

Community messaging

Unchanged by Fable in this pass, included so the whole journey is visible.
